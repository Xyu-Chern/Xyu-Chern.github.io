R1Oct 2025 – May 2026

Optimistic Multi-Step Value Estimation via Implicit Values ↗

Rare successful trajectories get diluted by a shared value network fitting mostly low-value targets — the credit assignment problem in sparse-reward RL.

  • Reinterpreted IQL to prioritize rare high-reward signals without catastrophic overestimation.
  • Developed IQ(λ) multi-step Q estimator and IV(λ), a lightweight plug-in replacement for GAE.
  • Coded the offline environment, ran experiments, and proposed the long-horizon idea from highway learning.

Result: IV beats PPO in 7 / 10 sparse-reward and 7 / 8 dense-reward tasks; Empty-16×16 ≈0.9 vs 0.5, DoorKey-8×8 >0.5 vs ≈0.

R2Jun 2025 – Feb 2026

Unsupervised Exploration with Expected Deep Information Gain

Neural dynamics models lack a tractable Bayesian posterior, so information-gain exploration usually relies on expensive or biased approximations.

  • Parameterized a conjugate prior and next-state likelihood with neural networks for efficient information gain.
  • Computed information gain via analytical KL divergence and provided a convergence analysis under stated assumptions.
  • Proposed the initial idea, ran sub-environments, and contributed the convergence analysis.

Result: DIG-collected data gave the best IQL results in 10 / 14 Gymnax tasks; in 7 tasks unsupervised DIG matched SAC trained with task rewards.

R3Jun 2024 – Sep 2025

Offline-to-Online RL Strategy Optimization via Diffusion Modeling (DOAL) ↗

Backpropagating Q-value gradients through diffusion / flow action generation is expensive and unstable in offline RL.

  • Proposed DOAL (Direct Optimal Action Learning) so varied policy classes can use Q-values for action improvement.
  • Applied DOAL to Gaussian, diffusion and flow policies with Max Q Action Sampling; established BC / IQL baselines.
  • Reinterpreted the Q-loss / behavior-loss balance as a trust region, yielding a Batch-Normalizing Optimizer.

Result: Consistent gains over strong baselines across 15 OGBench and D4RL Adroit tasks with less task-specific hyperparameter tuning.
